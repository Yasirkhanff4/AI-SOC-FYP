# Chapter 6 — Conclusion and Future Work

## 6.1 Conclusion

AI-SOC demonstrates a complete defensive workflow from security event ingestion through detection, risk scoring, investigation assistance, enrichment, MITRE mapping, incident response, and reporting. Its modular design lets a student team explain both engineering decisions and operational trade-offs.

The final conclusion must reference only measured tests and observed demonstrations. It must not claim that the system detects all attacks or replaces trained analysts.
